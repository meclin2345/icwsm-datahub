      How this catalog was made

      Each paper was read by an AI model (Claude) against a 65-field codebook, with a verbatim quote from the paper backing every key claim. The quotes, links and DOIs were then checked automatically against the paper text: about 9 in 10 quotes and 97% of dataset links were found word for word. Records the checks flagged were reviewed by hand. Finally every dataset link, DOI and repository record was tested live, on the audit date. A link can be down for a day, so treat a single failed check as a hint, not a verdict.

      Something wrong? Corrections are logged as documented overrides, so every fix is traceable. Suggest a correction.
