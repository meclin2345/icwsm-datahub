# Data license

The catalog files in `catalog/` and `docs/data/` are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The catalog describes papers published in the Proceedings of the International AAAI Conference on Web and Social Media. The papers themselves are not redistributed here and remain under their publisher's terms; short evidence quotes are included for verification and scholarly commentary.
